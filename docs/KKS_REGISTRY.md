# Kavsar Knowledge Register

**Purpose:**  
This register is the master index of permanent organizational knowledge within Kavsar Platform and the broader Kavsar ecosystem.

Every significant constitutional principle, architectural decision, standard, playbook, business rule, or permanent record shall receive a unique KP identifier.

---

## Identifier Policy

Every permanent knowledge record receives:

- A universal **KP ID**
- A document type
- A title
- A status
- An owner
- A date
- A proposal originator
- An approving authority
- The reasoning behind the decision
- Alternatives considered
- Consequences
- Related records
- Historical context where appropriate

Document-specific identifiers may also be used for readability.

Examples:

- KP-0002 / ADR-002
- KP-0008 / STD-001
- KP-0014 / PB-003

The KP identifier remains the universal reference across the Kavsar ecosystem.

---

## Governance

The Founder retains final approval authority over permanent knowledge records.

The Chief Software Architect serves initially as the Custodian of Knowledge and is responsible for:

- identifying decisions that require permanent recording,
- maintaining consistency between records,
- preventing duplicate or conflicting knowledge,
- recommending the appropriate record type,
- preserving the reasoning and historical context behind important decisions.
